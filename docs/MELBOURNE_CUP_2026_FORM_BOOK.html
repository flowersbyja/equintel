Joseph O'Brien (Sons And Lovers, Trustyourinstinct, Goodie Two Shoes, James J Braddock, Kizlyar)

Method. Everything is done in Ireland. The Werribee routine is kept simple and short. He is a positional rider's trainer: Twilight Payment led throughout in 2020 and Master Of Reality raced on the pace in 2019, while Rekindling, Okita Soushi and Goodie Two Shoes were ridden midfield and produced late. He will run a lightly raced classic-generation horse (Rekindling 3yo, Master Of Reality 4yo, now James J Braddock) and he will bring the same older horse back year after year.
