## Caveats

- Historical prep templates for 2000-2009 and for some 2nd-4th placegetters are reconstructed from form memory and down-weighted via a confidence field; treat named similarities as indicative.
- Lead-up tempo ratings are categorical (slow / moderate / fast) and were set to be consistent with Racelab's published count that only 11 of the 24 runners came through a moderate-or-faster lead-up.
- Prices are a consensus snapshot; re-run with updated odds and the final track rating on race morning.
- This is a research model, not betting advice. Probabilities are calibrated loosely by shrinking the market, not by a fitted backtest, because there is one Cup a year.
